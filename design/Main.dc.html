The doubt everyone has

“What happens when the company shuts the servers off?”

Nothing. That is the whole point. Every rule you write is stored and executed on the hub you own. If Smarteck disappeared tomorrow, your lights would still come on at sunset.

The remote-access app is the only part that touches our servers, and it is optional — turn it off and the hub is an island that answers only to your network.

Nothing leaves the house

Motion, occupancy and camera events are processed on the hub. The network log is yours to read.

No subscription, ever

You buy the hardware once. Firmware and rule updates are free for the life of the hub.

Open when you outgrow us

Local API, documented rule format, and a one-click export. No lock-in worth the name.
